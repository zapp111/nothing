# How to Play — Mushak: The Festive Quest

## Objective
Help Mushak collect puja offerings and bring them back to the pandal before time runs out!

Each stage gives you a specific task:
- **Stage 1:** Collect 4 Modaks, 3 Flowers, 2 Durva (Total 12)
- **Stage 2:** Collect 6 Modaks, 4 Flowers, 4 Durva (Total 18)
- **Stage 3:** Collect 8 Modaks, 6 Flowers, 5 Durva (Total 24)

After collecting, survive until timer ends to reach the Pandal!

## Controls

### Desktop / Laptop
- **A / D** or **← / →** — Move left/right
- **SPACE** — Activate Ganesha's Blessing (when ready)
- **P** — Pause / Resume
- **R** — Restart stage
- **Mouse** — Click buttons, drag Mushak on canvas

### Mobile
- **Drag finger** on canvas to move Mushak directly (best)
- **◀ ▶ buttons** — Move left/right
- **🙏 button** — Activate Blessing (glows when ready)
- Works in portrait and landscape

## Collectibles

| Item | Points | Blessing | Tip |
|------|--------|----------|-----|
| 🍬 Modak | +10 | +10 | Basic, common |
| 🌸 Flower | +15 | +14 | Festival offering |
| 🌿 Durva | +20 | +18 | Sacred grass |
| ✨ Golden Modak | +100 | +30 | Rare! In risky spots near obstacles |

**Golden Modaks:** Appear near barriers — high risk, high reward!

## Obstacles
- 🪨 Stones, 📦 Boxes, 🚧 Barriers, 🪣 Buckets
- Hit = -1 life, combo resets, 1.6s invulnerability (flicker)
- 3 lives: ❤️❤️❤️ → ❤️❤️🤍 → ❤️🤍🤍 → Game Over

## Combo System
Collect without hitting obstacles to build combo:

- 1-2: ×1
- 3-5: ×2 — "COMBO x2!"
- 6-9: ×3 — "COMBO x3!!"
- 10-14: ×4 — "COMBO x4!!! 🔥"
- 15-21: ×5 — "COMBO x5!!!!! 🌟"
- 22+: ×6 — "GODLIKE x6!!!! 🌟🌟"

**Multiplier boosts ALL points!** Keep collecting to stay high!

## Ganesha's Blessing 🙏
- **Fill meter:** Collect offerings (gold bar at top)
- **When 100%:** "READY! PRESS SPACE" + glow + sound
- **Activate:** Press SPACE or 🙏 button
- **Effects (6.5s):**
  - Game slows to 42% (easier to dodge)
  - Score multiplier ×1.5 +1
  - Invincible to obstacles
  - Golden aura with ॐ + particles
  - Looks special, respectful (not attack)

**Strategy:** Save for difficult sections or when many Golden Modaks appear!

## Stages

### 🛕 Stage 1 — The Pandal (Easy)
- Duration: 45s
- Speed: 320→480
- Learn movement, basic collecting, first blessing
- Objective: 12 offerings

### 🥁 Stage 2 — The Procession (Medium)
- Duration: 55s
- Speed: 420→620
- More lanes, more obstacles, more golden modaks
- Procession decorations, faster
- Objective: 18 offerings

### 🌊 Stage 3 — The Final Journey (Hard)
- Duration: 65s
- Speed: 520→780
- Highest speed, hardest patterns, risky golden placements
- Full blessing usage needed
- Objective: 24 offerings

## Scoring
- **Base:** Sum of collectible values × combo multiplier
- **Time Bonus:** Remaining time × 12 (final) + 200 per stage
- **Perfect Stage:** +500 if objective fully completed
- **Golden Bonus:** +50 per Golden Modak (final)
- **Best Score:** Saved locally, try to beat it!

## Tips
1. **Stay centered** initially, then move to collect
2. **Don't chase every item** — protect combo and lives
3. **Golden Modaks are risky** — only go if you have lives + blessing ready
4. **Use Blessing wisely** — when screen is crowded or golden appears
5. **Drag on mobile** is more precise than buttons
6. **Watch timer** — when <6s, finish line appears! If objective done, you win!
7. **Build combo early** — early ×2 makes mid-game much higher score

## Respectful Note
This game is respectfully themed around Ganesh Chaturthi. Lord Ganesha is not depicted being hurt or in disrespectful situations. Blessing is a respectful aura, not a weapon. All festival elements are celebratory.

## Troubleshooting
- **Images not loading?** Game falls back to canvas drawing, still playable
- **No sound?** Click 🔊 toggle, check browser audio permission, interact first (browser policy)
- **Laggy?** Close other tabs, check FPS display, try lower resolution (zoom out)
- **Mobile not moving?** Try drag instead of buttons, ensure touch not blocked
