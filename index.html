📖 HOW TO PLAY

🎮
Move Mushak
A / D or ← / →
Mobile: Drag finger or ◀ ▶
🍬
Collect Offerings
Modak +10, Flower +15, Durva +20, Golden +100 — Golden in risky spots!
🚧
Avoid Obstacles
Stones, boxes, barriers. Hit = -1 life + combo reset + 1.6s invulnerability
🔥
Build Combo
3→×2, 6→×3, 10→×4, 15→×5, 22→×6. Multiplier boosts all points!
🙏
Ganesha's Blessing
Fill meter (gold bar). When READY press SPACE / 🙏 button. Slows time 58%, +1.5× multiplier, invincible 6.5s!
🎯
Objective
Each stage needs specific offerings. Collect them & survive till timer ends. Time <3s shows Pandal finish line!
⭐
Scoring
Base + Combo + Time Bonus + Perfect Stage (+500) + Golden Bonus (+50 each). Save best score locally!
📱
Mobile
Touch & drag Mushak directly, or use ◀ ▶. Blessing button glows when ready. Works in portrait & landscape!
